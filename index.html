About Me

I am a junior full-stack & Ai engineer with a growing passion for building practical, real-world web applications that solve meaningful problems. With a background in Psychology and Business, I bring a unique perspective to software development, combining an understanding of human behavior, critical thinking, and business strategy to create user-centered digital experiences.

My journey into tech is driven by curiosity and a genuine desire to understand how the digital products we use every day are designed and built. Every project I undertake is an opportunity to strengthen my technical skills while gaining a deeper understanding of what works and what doesn't. What inspires me most about web development is its ability to transform ideas into accessible solutions with global reach.

As I continue to polish my expertise in front-end and back-end technologies, my goal is to become a well-rounded software & Ai engineer capable of designing, developing, and deploying impactful digital products. I am committed to continuous learning, embracing new technologies, and contributing to projects that make a positive difference for individuals, businesses, and communities.
